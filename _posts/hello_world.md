### We are gamifying decentralized finance to enable uniquely secure trading by transforming toxic MEV incentives into free market opportunities, internalizing them into better prices.


The principle of <strong><a href="https://urani.trade/" target="_blank">Urani Protocol and Swap</a></strong> is straightforward: it harnesses the power of <strong><a href="https://docs.urani.trade/urani-swap/technical-considerations/uranis-intents" target="_blank">intents</a></strong>, a type of order flow auction mechanism where a user submits a signed message containing information for the Urani Protocol to execute the trade on their behalf.

Additionally, inspired by the world of extreme sports, where enmity and friendship coexist amidst thrilling moments, <strong>Urani Arena</strong> provides a *real-time* visual infrastructure for orderflow auction competition.


<br>

<div class="quote-box">
MEV, short for Maximum Extractable Value, represents profit opportunities from on-chain transactions. 
  
**Arbitrage is generally considered a healthy type of MEV as it promotes a free market.** Conversely, toxic or harmful MEV can occur when users trade with high slippage and unknowingly become victims of sandwich attacks, or when the Solana network is congested due to heavy sniping.

Additionally, we coined the term "MEV agents" to distinguish "healthy" bots from toxic MEV bots. We refer to the humans behind these bots as "MEV agent operators." At Urani, they are as important as any other actor in the supply chain.



**Anyone can become an MEV agent operator and build upon their favorite strategy to win order batches**. We call this "bring your favorite strategy" (BYFS). You can think of agents as "customizable skateboards", allowing operators to navigate the hypergraph of the order flow auction landscape. 

The competition runs for a little less than a second, and the winning operator takes a part of the surplus (the rest goes back to the users).
